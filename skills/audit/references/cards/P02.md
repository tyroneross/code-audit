# P02 — Make builds, checks, and releases repeatable

When a build, check, or delivery step must be repeated or handed off, make it reproducible and add automation only when its recurring benefit justifies maintenance.

Read [audit interpretation](../audit-interpretation.json) first. Review unsafe uses and unsupported claims even when a safe technique prerequisite is absent. Adapt validation examples to the actual contract; combine or stage options when useful.

## Purpose and mechanism

Reproduce important changes, failures, and delivery operations reliably.

Versioned inputs, repeatable builds, and concrete defect evidence let different people or agents reproduce the same operation.

## Context

**Source applicability:** Changes recur, several contributors work on the product, setup knowledge is implicit, or a reported defect cannot be reproduced.

**Avoid:** A working repository workflow already supplies the needed evidence; do not create a competing build or tracking system for one task.

**Premises:**
- The artifact will be maintained, reproduced, or handed off.
- Automation will support a recurring operation rather than add an unused parallel process.

## Options and costs

### Follow documented steps

When: The operation is rare, short, and easy for another person to repeat.

Action: Record the inputs and steps, perform the check, and keep the result.

Gain: Makes the operation repeatable without maintaining automation.

Cost: People can omit steps or use different inputs.

### Script the repeated step

When: Setup or validation is repeated often enough that manual steps are being missed or performed differently.

Action: Reuse existing tools and automate the repeated step that causes errors or wasted effort.

Gain: Reduces variation without building a second delivery system.

Cost: Someone must maintain the script and its assumptions about the environment.

### Use an integrated pipeline

When: Several contributors repeatedly build, check, and deliver changes that others depend on.

Action: Connect the required build, verification, and delivery steps in the existing pipeline.

Gain: Runs the same checks consistently and catches omissions earlier.

Cost: The pipeline needs maintenance and can delay feedback or delivery when it fails.

## Examples

Example: A packaging fix must be reproducible outside the developer's configured machine.

Counterexample: Saving a uniquely named research document does not require constructing a CI pipeline or a new defect database.

## Adapt when assumptions change

- A one-off experiment becomes a maintained component. Add repeatable setup and checks before multiple consumers depend on it.
- An existing pipeline already covers the changed boundary. Run and reference it instead of building another pipeline or tracking surface.

## Context-adapted validation probes

- The intended build or check works from the documented starting state.
- Another contributor can reproduce a material defect or the evidence explains why it remains uncertain.
