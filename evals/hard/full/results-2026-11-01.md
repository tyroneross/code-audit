# Full-guidance arm: results, 2026-11-01

The checklist and the 30 principle cards did not help. With them, Code Audit detected exactly what the bundle-only arm, both sources and the no-guidance control detected: 6 of 12 primary review trials, for every arm. Every arm still missed H01, H07 and H08, even though checklist item C3 describes H08's mechanism almost word for word and cards P14 and P19 cover H01. The full bundle cost about 1.7 times the tokens of the bundle-only arm.

## Verdict (pre-registered rule, primary set)

`code-audit-full` against each comparator: parity (Δ 0.00 on review, Δ 0 on repair). The claim that the full guidance helps did not hold.

## What this says

- **Prose rules did not move the model on these cases.** The full bundle spelled out the mechanism behind H08 (C3: "A timeout is not proof nothing happened") and the failure classes behind H01 (P14, P19). The model still did not connect them to the specific code. Reviewers almost always cited the defect lines; they described a different problem there.
- **Cost.** About 1.7 times the tokens of the bundle-only arm, with no measured benefit on this benchmark.
- **Scope.** This is one model (gpt-6.1-sol at medium effort) on tool-less reviews of 10 packets. An agent that loads references on demand, runs the scanner or uses tools was not measured. The scan-hit variant was not run.

## Validity and limits

- **Validity.** All 180 trials completed. The final audit flagged none of the 60 repair trials.
- **Judging.** Two blinded Opus-tier judges graded the 12 case packets. One applied a keyword rule written after reading every finding, then hand-checked two packets against it. Each finding was judged once.
- **Sample size.** Two repetitions over six primary cases: one trial is 0.083.
