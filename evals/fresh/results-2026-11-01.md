# Fresh-case benchmark results (2026-11-01)

The adaptive methods did not detect more defects than any comparison arm. Under the pre-registered analysis, `adaptive` detected 17 of the 46–47 fresh defects valid for it. Every primary comparison is **parity**, with Holm-adjusted p = 1.0:
- against `none+tools`, `bundle+tools` and `principles+tools`, the two arms disagreed on no case;
- against `fixed-plan`, `adaptive` missed 1 case that `fixed-plan` found.

Tools did not help either: `none+tools` detected 18/48 paired cases where the tool-less `none` detected 21, at 17× the tokens. The instrument itself works: the positive control separated at p = 8.0e-7.

Protocol: [README.md](README.md) and Addenda 1–3, committed before the trials or outputs they govern. Raw trials, judge packets, judgments and aggregates are in `.build-loop/evals/fresh-r1*`, `fresh-r2*` and `fresh-stats-*.json` (untracked).

## Instrument validity

- **Positive control.** `hint` detected 46/54 and `none` 22/54: 25 vs 1 discordant cases, exact McNemar p = 8.0e-7. The instrument detects a large planted effect.
- **Discrimination.** `none` at 0.41 and the tool arms at 0.35–0.38 are below the 0.75 ceiling.
- **Judging.** Opus-tier judges graded 124 blinded packets holding 1,075 findings: 8 judges in round 1 and 12 in round 2. Every finding got a row. Judge assignments and transcripts are not kept on disk.
  - **Spot checks.** 8 random decisions agreed with the oracle on 7. One accept was lenient, and each case's packet held every arm's findings, so that leniency applied to all arms alike.
  - **False-negative check.** Round-2 reasons repeat within packets, so an independent auditor asked whether judges labelled findings in uniform batches. A fresh blind Opus judge re-graded 30 randomly sampled round-2 non-matches whose primary location sat inside the defect window (seed 20261101, 87 eligible). It agreed with all 30. The false-negative rate among such rows is likely below about 10%.
- **Manipulation check.** It took. `adaptive` and `fixed-plan` ran `scan.py` and `recon.py` in 62/62 trials. `none+tools` ran `scan.py` in 19/62 and `recon.py` in 32/62; `bundle+tools` ran `scan.py` in 18/62. The methods changed what the agents ran, not what they found.

**Network-flagged trials.** The `evals/hard` network audit flagged 33 tool-arm trials across all five arms, 4–8 each.
- **Inspection.** Every flagged command was a probe in which a model ran the repository's own command classifier or hook on test strings, for example `'git push'`, `npm install lodash --before=…` and `https://example.test`. None ran a network tool or opened a connection.
- **Bias.** The audit penalizes probing, which `adaptive` asks for. That bias runs against the arm under test, and it did not decide the outcome.

## What this does and does not show

- **Shows.** At medium effort, with tools and a whole repository, `gpt-6.1-sol` detected the same defects with and without code-audit's methods, its bundle, or the source plugin's bundle, within 0–2 discordant cases. Tools alone did not raise detection over the tool-less packet, and cost about 17× the tokens and 2.6× the time. A second review pass added 2 cases (p = 0.5).
- **Does not show.**
  - **Effects on other models or higher effort.** Claude-hosted reviewer arms were blocked: no API key exists.
  - **Fan-out and lane designs.** Every arm was a single agent.
  - **Defects outside the oracle.** Detection credits only the mined defect, and finding counts differ: `adaptive` reported 183 findings against 209 for `none+tools` in the sensitivity analysis.
  - **Effects smaller than about 6 net discordant cases in 54.**
- **Interpretation** [INFERRED]. For this model and these cases, detection depends almost entirely on the case, not on the procedure. The untested levers are model, effort, multi-pass or parallel review at larger scale, and per-case facts such as a class hint. The hint moved detection by 0.44.

## Disclosures

- **Labels seen.** The orchestrator saw each case's key and one-line class label before the methods were frozen. One mining lane's labels described part of each mechanism. The methods text predates the cases, and its authoring lane did not read them.
- **Harness reruns.** 15 tool-arm trials on F42–F44 failed before the model ran, because an absolute symlink in the decision-doctor archive stopped extraction.
  - The classifier of the day recorded them as `invalid_response`, so `run.py set-aside` did not apply. They were moved aside by hand and rerun once.
  - The fix was in the working tree, uncommitted, when the reruns ran; it was committed in 3a63677.
  - 7 of the 15 reruns were then network-flagged.
- **Tool sources.** Two runner processes served round 2. The first copied tools from the worktree, which was clean at de06478 for the whole run. The second wrote them from git at de06478; the 110 trials it served record matching hashes.
- **Post-registration text fix.** The fresh README's "Located" wording was corrected after round 1 to match the code's ±10-line tolerance, in a909d6b. The metric itself did not change.
- **Single run.** One repetition per case, one judge per packet.
