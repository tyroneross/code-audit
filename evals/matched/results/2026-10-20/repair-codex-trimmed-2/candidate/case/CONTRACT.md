Checkout.execute(operation_id,payload,total_timeout) coordinates a logical operation and returns the provider receipt. A caller may repeat the SAME operation_id and payload after an uncertain response without creating a second charge. A different operation_id is a new action. Reusing an ID with a different payload must surface ValueError without a new charge. The operation must not exceed total_timeout on the injected clock. At most max_attempts provider attempts may occur in an execution; retries cannot hide at another layer. If no receipt is obtained before budget/attempt exhaustion, raise TimeoutError; expiry does not mean no charge happened. Gateway.submit is one provider attempt; it accepts a stable operation_id and optional timeout. Standalone calls without an operation_id create a new identity once per invocation. Policy.per_attempt(remaining=None) selects a positive timeout from profile data or its explicit assumed_timeout when data is absent. Profiles supply warm_p99, cold_p99 and network_allowance; ordinary cold behavior plus allowance must fit when the caller's budget permits, and changing the relevant profile must change the selected limit. A smaller remaining budget caps the chosen limit, and exhausted/nonpositive budgets must not start work. The published max_attempts is configurable; preserve return shape, injected clock/transport and existing entry points.
